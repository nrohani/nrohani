POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCH ASSOCIATE · UNIVERSITY OF OXFORD

Narjes Rohani

My research focuses on the molecular mechanisms of brain disorders and on turning that mechanistic insight into therapeutic opportunities. I integrate multi-omic data from developing human brain tissue with computational genomics and machine learning to resolve how genetic risk is translated through gene-regulatory programs across time and cell types — with particular emphasis on cis-regulatory elements in both common and rare neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders.

RESEARCH

Research focus

01 · CURRENT

Brain disorders and therapy

I analyse large-scale multi-omic data from developing brain tissue to understand when and how disease mechanisms emerge, and to identify cis-regulatory elements that may be therapeutically actionable. The long-term goal is to make precision medicine for brain disorders more biologically grounded and more tractable.

Read more →
02 · EARLIER

Computational drug & disease prediction

My earlier work developed AI and machine-learning models for drug–drug interaction prediction, drug–disease association, and breast-cancer molecular subtyping — building a computational foundation for bioinformatics and biomedical data analysis.

Read more →
03 · TRANSLATION & EDUCATION

Precision medicine, learning & public engagement

During my PhD I studied AI and learning analytics in precision medicine and health data science. I also develop accessible science communication, including Genes, Brains & Breakthroughs, an animated series explaining neurodevelopmental disorders and genomic research to patients and families.
