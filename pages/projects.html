CURRENT

Neurodevelopmental & psychiatric genomics

PROGRAMME · ONGOING

Genomic & epigenetic basis of neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders

scRNA-seqscATAC-seqBulk DNA-seqAutism & schizophrenia

Question: When during brain development, and through which regulatory DNA regions, do the genetic risk factors for autism, schizophrenia, and related conditions begin to act?

Approach: Integrating gene expression, chromatin accessibility, and DNA sequencing from human brain tissue across developmental stages, in the Sanders and Rinaldi labs at Oxford's IDRM, and applying machine learning to age-resolved multi-omic data to trace disease-relevant gene regulation over developmental time.

Selected current projects

BrainVar programme

International collaboration integrating single-nucleus RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and whole-genome sequencing from human brain tissue across development, including a dedicated analysis of sex bias in gene regulation. Manuscripts in preparation.

Enhancer discovery for therapeutic targeting

Combining chromatin accessibility and expression data to prioritise candidate regulatory elements — including enhancers linked to repeat-expansion genes — for neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disease genes.

Retinoic acid & cortical arealization

Contributed computational analyses to a study identifying a retinoic acid autoregulatory loop governing prefrontal-motor cortex development, published in Nature.

Poison exons & haploinsufficiency

Designed and performed the computational analyses supporting a study on poison exons and haploinsufficiency mechanisms, accepted in Genome Medicine.

Whole-brain Perturb-seq mapping

Contributed to genome-scale functional mapping of the mammalian brain using in vivo Perturb-seq, accepted in Nature.

Friedreich's ataxia (FXN/TJP2)

Leading computational analyses for a funded study of Friedreich's ataxia, including identification of a long TJP2 transcript as a candidate contributor to disease mechanism.

Neurodegenerative disease genomics

Computational analyses supporting collaborative studies of SBMA and RNA editing in neurodegenerative disease, in preparation for submission.

Genes, Brains & Breakthroughs (PPIE)

Principal Investigator of a public engagement project translating neurodevelopmental genomics into accessible animations for patients, families, and clinicians. See Public engagement →

Machine learning for drug interaction & cancer subtyping

Neural networksMatrix factorizationBioinformatics

Question: Can integrated similarity measures between drugs, and deep-clustering of tumour profiles, predict interactions and disease subtypes that haven't yet been observed clinically?

Approach: Built neural-network and matrix-factorization models for drug–drug interaction prediction, and a deep-clustering approach for breast cancer molecular subtyping; contributed computational analyses on CDK4/6 inhibitor resistance in breast cancer as a research visitor at the Institute of Cancer Research, London.
