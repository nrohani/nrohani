Public engagement

I want science to be accessible to the people who need it most, not only to scientists. My training in how people learn complex concepts informs how I communicate genomic and neurodevelopmental research: breaking difficult ideas into clear, engaging explanations while preserving the biology and the uncertainty that matter.
