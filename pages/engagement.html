PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT

Public engagement

Dr. Rohani is glad to talk about genomics, AI, and the developing brain with any audience — from patient groups to computer scientists curious about biology. Get in touch to invite a talk, contribute to an event, or collaborate on outreach.

Talks & seminars

Invited talks on computational genomics, neurodevelopmental disease, and machine learning in biology, including FENS Forum, the SLC6A1 and SCN2A community conferences, and departmental seminars.

Science communication

Research updates and commentary on genomics and AI are shared on X / Twitter.

Collaboration & outreach

Enquiries about partnering on outreach connecting computer science and biology students, or on public communication of neurodevelopmental research, are welcome by email.

PPIE PROJECT · PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Genes, Brains & Breakthroughs

A 16-part animated video series translating neurodevelopmental genomics and gene therapy into clear, family-friendly explanations — co-created with patients, families, clinicians, and advocacy organisations, and led by Dr. Rohani as Principal Investigator.

Playing the full series from the @genebrainbreakthrough YouTube channel. Also on Instagram and TikTok.
